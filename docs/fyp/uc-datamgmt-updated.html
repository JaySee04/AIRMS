Use Case Diagram — Screening Data Management & Cohort Norms (superseded Module 4 grouping)

added since the FYP I decomposition  ·  dashed arrow = «include»
HoloMotion PDF is the sole screening import (the Excel import was retired 2026-07-12 and archived). Committing an import writes an immutable screening snapshot and then, in one debounced pass, recomputes the cohort norms, re-scores every athlete's overall risk indicator, and emails an alert to the medical staff and the athlete's sport coaches for anyone landing amber/red. Only approved cohorts drive the indicator.
