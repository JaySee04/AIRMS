A  ·  ACWR Calculation

How workload becomes a ratio

Session load (sRPE) Load (AU) = Duration (min) × RPE
↓
Acute load — last 7 days Acute = ∑ loads over rolling 7 days
↓
Chronic load — last 28 days Chronic = mean weekly load over 28 days
↓
ACWR ACWR = Acute / Chronic

Hardware-free: athlete logs duration and perceived effort — no GPS or HR strap required.

B  ·  Screening × ACWR → Risk

How screening shapes the bands

Vulnerability from screening (0–1) v = 0.50·ERS + 0.20·MobDef
    + 0.15·StbDef + 0.15·SymDef
Personalised ACWR thresholds factor = clamp(1 + (v − 0.4) × 0.4,  0.85,  1.15)
bands  =  0.8·f  /  1.3/f  /  1.5/f

Classify ACWR against the personalised bands:

> modMax  →  High
> lowMax  →  Moderate
≥ lowMin  →  Low

Then escalate one notch if active injuries or ≥5 muscle flags align with the current workload.

Foundational sRPE and ACWR evidence lives in Section 04 Literature Review. The personalisation and escalation layers above are AIRMS' contribution — reviewed and accepted by stakeholder Dr. Thung (ISN).

B  ·  Screening profile

From the athlete’s last screening

Uploaded by medical staff via Excel. Higher mobility / stability / symmetry is better; higher ERS is worse.

ERS Exercise Risk Score — composite from movement-test battery. Higher = more at risk 3 – 12 Mobility Joint range-of-motion score. Higher is better. 0 – 100 Stability Postural / core stability score. Higher is better. 0 – 100 Symmetry Left-vs-right balance score. Higher is better. 0 – 100 MobDef / StbDef / SymDef Their deficits: 1 − score/100. Flips them so higher = worse, matching ERS. v Vulnerability — the weighted composite of the four above. 0 = robust, 1 = highly vulnerable. 0 – 1
C  ·  Personalised thresholds

How the ACWR bands shift per athlete

The textbook Gabbett thresholds, stretched or tightened by each athlete’s vulnerability.

0.8 / 1.3 / 1.5 Textbook Gabbett thresholds — safe lower bound / safe upper bound / danger line 0.4 The vulnerability value treated as population baseline. At v = 0.4, personalised bands equal the textbook exactly. factor (f) Per-athlete multiplier. Vulnerable → f > 1 (tighter bands). Robust → f < 1 (wider bands). clamped ±15% lowMin 0.8 × f — below this = under-loaded lowMax 1.3 / f — top of the Low band modMax 1.5 / f — top of Moderate. Above = High Risk.
