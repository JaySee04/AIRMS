The General Module is cross-cutting and is not counted among the six functional modules. Injury & Recovery Logging (Module 2 in the previous decomposition) was removed 2026-08-02 under the HoloMotion-only directive: the system derives every signal from the screening instrument, so a parallel, manually maintained injury stream was inconsistent with that premise. Its six leaves, and the four injury-derived leaves elsewhere (Injury Overview, Filter Injury Data, Temporal Trends, Injury PDF, Sport-Level Context), are gone. All that survives is Set Athlete Injury Status — a clinician-set flag whose purpose is norm eligibility: an injured athlete should not shape the reference distribution their peers are judged against. To keep six modules, Module 2 is recast as Athlete Roster & Identity Management, gathering the master-data capabilities that were previously scattered and unenumerated (athlete registration keyed by identity-card number, roster maintenance and search, event vocabulary) together with the institute-directory lookup. Earlier: Activity Tracking & Logging (Module 1 in the FYP I decomposition) was removed 2026-07-20; the composite formula (classifyCompositeRisk()) is preserved in lib/risk.ts as a locked decision but has no live caller. The cohort-normed overall risk indicator (Module 1) is the system's single risk verdict.  * The Coach is a first-class fourth role (read-only, sport-scoped, FYP II) alongside athlete / medical / administrator.  † The institute directory is demonstrated against a stand-in source, built as a swappable integration seam pending ISN access.
