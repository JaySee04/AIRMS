The General Module is cross-cutting and is not counted among the six functional modules. Module 1 in the FYP I decomposition (Activity Tracking & Logging) was fully removed 2026-07-20, at JC's request — its ACWR/composite-risk display had already left every dashboard on 2026-07-16, and with nothing left to surface its output, the module itself (plus the Prevention Insight and Recovery Baseline leaves that depended on it) was removed rather than kept as dead weight. To keep six modules, the old "Screening Data Management & Cohort Norms" module (the largest, 10 leaves) was split into Module 3 (Screening Data Ingestion) and Module 4 (Cohort Norms & Governance) — the latter also gains "View Screening Cohort Analytics", moved in from Analytics & Reporting. The composite formula (classifyCompositeRisk()) is preserved in lib/risk.ts as a locked decision but has no live caller. The cohort-normed overall risk indicator (Module 1) is the system's single risk verdict.  * The Coach is a first-class fourth role (read-only, FYP II) alongside athlete / medical / administrator.
