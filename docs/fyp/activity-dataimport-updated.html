Activity Diagram — Screening Data Import (HoloMotion PDF, batch)

Shaded band = runs entirely on the operator's machine; nothing inside it reaches an external service
Redaction precedes transmission. The report's pages are rendered and the athlete's name is located and obscured entirely on the operator's machine; only then is an image sent to the vision model, which therefore receives a complete but anonymous report. If the name cannot be located confidently, the whole identifying region is obscured rather than risking disclosure. Re-attachment is also local. Because the name is gone from the extracted data, the athlete is proposed from the PDF's own filename — read in the browser, never uploaded — and confirmed or corrected by the operator; so the link between identity and assessment is never transmitted either. Nothing is written until the operator confirms, and the cohort recompute runs once after the queue drains rather than per file, so importing a batch of N reports triggers one re-scoring pass, not N.
